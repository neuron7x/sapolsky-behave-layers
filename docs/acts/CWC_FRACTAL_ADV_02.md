# CWC-FRACTAL-ADV-02 — Cross-Seed Replication Veto / Programme Synthesis

**Status:** FROZEN BEFORE PROGRAMME-LEVEL SYNTHESIS EXECUTION  
**Scope:** evidence synthesis only; fresh traces already exist and are immutable inputs  
**Scientific ascension authority:** none  
**VIA authority:** none

## 1. Why this act exists

The three model seeds (101, 211, 307) were frozen before fresh trace generation in
`CWC_FRACTAL_ADV_01`. Individual-trace tests can nevertheless produce an isolated positive by
sampling variation. A generalized multiscale claim must therefore survive a replication attack.

This act is intentionally **one-way fail-closed**. It is written after the individual trace results
exist, so it may veto or narrow a claim but MUST NOT upgrade the evidence to confirmatory positive
support. Any positive programme-level statistic produced here remains exploratory and requires a new
prospective replication.

## 2. Immutable inputs

Exactly the three variable-shape fresh traces are admissible:

- seed 101, 72 records;
- seed 211, 72 records;
- seed 307, 72 records.

The three fixed-shape traces are retained as identifiability probes. They are not silently pooled with
variable-shape traces because their residual pair coverage differs structurally.

## 3. Replication veto

The programme records:

1. number of variable-shape seeds whose frozen per-trace primary gate passed;
2. sign and magnitude of every residual pair association across seeds;
3. fixed-shape identifiability rate;
4. a pooled cross-seed null diagnostic.

A generalized support claim is **vetoed** if fewer than two of the three prespecified variable-shape
seeds independently pass the already-frozen v2 trace gate. This 2/3 rule is a post-hoc conservative
veto only: satisfying it would not authorize a positive scientific claim.

## 4. Pooled null diagnostic

For characterization, define the programme statistic as the arithmetic mean of each seed's mean
absolute residual Spearman coherence. In every null iteration, transform targets independently within
each seed, compute the seed statistic, average across seeds, and take the maximum statistic across the
three frozen null families. Use 5,000 Monte-Carlo iterations.

This pooled diagnostic cannot rescue a failed replication veto and cannot authorize scientific
ascension because its synthesis rule was not preregistered before trace inspection.

## 5. Claim boundary

Permitted conclusions are implementation/evidence boundaries only:

- replicated residual multiscale association is or is not supported by the current instrument;
- observed associations are stable or unstable across model seeds;
- shared shape can or cannot explain particular raw associations.

Forbidden conclusions remain: fractal cognition, intelligence, biological equivalence, causal
hierarchy, useful adaptive computation, or physical GPU efficiency.
