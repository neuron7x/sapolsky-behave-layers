# DGC Metareasoning Prior-Art Boundary

- Stuart Russell, Eric Wefald, *Principles of Metareasoning*, Artificial Intelligence 49 (1991). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(91)90015-C
- Nicholas Hay, Stuart Russell, David Tolpin, Solomon Eyal Shimony, *Selecting Computations: Theory and Applications*, UAI 2012 / arXiv:1207.5879. https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.5879

These works establish that decision-theoretic selection of computations and value-of-computation are prior art. DGC's contribution claim must therefore be limited to its particular engineering composition, governance contracts, falsification program and any later empirically demonstrated properties. No theorem-novelty claim is authorized from the current evidence.
