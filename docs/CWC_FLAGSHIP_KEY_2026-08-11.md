# CWC Flagship-Key Analysis — 2026-08-11

## Reverse objective

The external objective is not to maximize repository size or the number of internal
mechanisms. A memorable research contribution needs one compact claim that another
researcher can understand, attack, reproduce, and use without knowing the CWC history.


## Current programme boundary after direct route falsification

The learned two-exit adaptive-depth route lineage is now closed: R1 shared routing was
`NOT_SUPPORTED`, and the single preregistered per-model-calibration rescue R2 also returned
`NOT_SUPPORTED` (PRIMARY `5/6`, REPLICATION `4/6`; no R3 permitted). This removes adaptive-depth
Pareto routing as the current flagship candidate. The thesis below is therefore the sole surviving
scientific flagship and must stand or fall on `REAL-TRANSFER-01`; internal routing evidence cannot
rescue it.

## Current strongest candidate thesis

> **Do not spend computation identifying a causal world when the surviving worlds are
> decision-equivalent. Preserve causal ambiguity explicitly, act only when the decision
> is invariant across it, and acquire evidence only against distinctions capable of
> changing the decision.**

This is a candidate flagship *synthesis*, not a novelty claim.

## Why this dominates the immediate alternatives

1. `COG-WORLDS-02` would broaden the countermodel class but by itself produces another
   internal mechanism rather than an externally legible result.
2. More causal-credit estimator work is downstream of already-discovered structural and
   intervention-semantic limits.
3. Large-scale Pareto evaluation is important eventually, but the real-workload line has
   frozen negatives and local hardware does not currently support a defensible frontier
   result.
4. Proof-carrying planning, memory and typed epistemics now exist, so the missing piece is
   a decision-level principle that turns those safety primitives into measurable value.
5. The new COG-INFO-02 synthetic result exposes a concrete inefficiency in the previous
   model-identification governor: same-decision countermodels can block useful information
   acquisition even though they cannot change the action.

## Scientific compression

Let `M_D` be the surviving model set and `g(M)` the optimal action induced by a model.
The object that matters for the immediate decision is not model identity `M` but the
quotient `M_D / ~g` where models are equivalent when they recommend the same action.

Three regimes follow:

1. one decision cell -> act robustly while causal truth remains unresolved;
2. multiple cells + discriminating evidence -> buy only decision-relevant information;
3. multiple cells + zero admissible decision information -> abstain / declare the
   decision unidentified.

This creates a falsifiable engineering target: **decision resolution per unit compute at
fixed false-authority rate**, not attribution quality or raw model-identification rate.

## External literature pressure

The thesis must be positioned conservatively because its ingredients have strong prior
art: value of information and active test selection; decision-focused abstractions;
robust multivalued decision models; active causal discovery; decision-sufficient data;
Active Epistemic Control; and 2026 work decomposing causal-effect uncertainty into
sample-reducible versus non-identifiability components. CWC can only earn a distinct
contribution by demonstrating a new executable composition and a benchmark failure that
existing methods do not already solve.

## The actual 'legend gate'

Internal synthetic PASS is insufficient. The flagship survives only if the next public
benchmark shows, under matched compute:

- low false causal authority on observationally equivalent / confounded / operator-
  ambiguous cases;
- equal or better decision utility despite preserving more causal uncertainty;
- less wasted evidence acquisition than model-identification or generic uncertainty
  baselines;
- real-model transfer on at least two task families;
- independent third-party reproduction of the qualitative result.

If that fails, this thesis is not the key and must be killed rather than marketed.
