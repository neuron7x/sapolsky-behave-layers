## Literature and correction ledger

- Decision-region determination is a close prior result: Javdani et al., *Near Optimal Bayesian Active Learning for Decision Making*, AISTATS 2014, [primary paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v33/javdani14.html). Overlapping regions, necessary/sufficient containment and test selection have established precedent. No novelty claim for DS01 Theorems 1-3; compare hyperedge methods before claiming novelty.
- Blackwell, *Equivalent Comparisons of Experiments*, 1953, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177729032); Goel and DeGroot, *Comparison of Experiments and Information Measures*, 1979, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176344790): information comparisons and decision risks. Robust regret vs raw worst loss are not interchangeable; unique minimax is not common pointwise optimality.
- [Montiel Olea et al. partial-identification paper](https://joseluismontielolea.com/MQS.pdf) is a source lead from the supplied report; its admissibility theorem has model-specific assumptions, not a universal assertion for all partially identified decision problems.
- [QuantEcon exposition](https://python.quantecon.org/blackwell_kihlstrom.html) and [Sims research agenda](https://www.bundesbank.de/resource/blob/703284/82d389a1de62876b9151575e466767cd/mL/2005-09-27-dkp-34-data.pdf) are background leads. The quadratic value-of-information behavior and derivative optimality in the report are not general bounds for arbitrary acquisition. DS01 supplies an explicitly scoped combinatorial cost theorem instead.
- Expected posterior entropy decreases in expectation under a fixed correctly specified probabilistic model, not necessarily for every realized observation. Set refinement monotonicity requires a fixed hypothesis/measurement contract; model revision may widen the set.
- Numerical agreement of displayed decimals is not independent reproduction of a proof. Gate PASS is not scientific validity. Git commit identity is not a SHA-256 artifact digest; store both correctly.
- External-source audit verified the Javdani abstract, Blackwell bibliographic DOI, Goel-DeGroot article and the two dataset pages; other links are research leads, not an exhaustive literature survey.
