## 2. Final state and stop rule

- PASS: all original frozen endpoints, eligible matched-cost comparisons, complete two-family PRIMARY and REPLICATION evidence, no execution/provenance violation. Use exactly the contamination-limited authority in the original protocol.
- NOT_SUPPORTED: a valid execution falsifies a frozen required endpoint. A failed PRIMARY is terminal for this scientific flagship; replication cannot rescue it. Preserve the complete observed negative and stop tuning or replacing workloads.
- NOT_TESTED / BLOCKED: missing authentic inputs, inaccessible compute, unresolved source terms, invalid protocol execution, incomplete comparator roster, or absent required cost eligibility. Neither success nor refutation. Preserve exact blocker; stop unbounded retries. If the existing protocol has a defect, record it separately, do not reinterpret after seeing PRIMARY.
- Engineering completion is distinct from the scientific verdict. A valid negative can close the research programme at 100% completion. A blocker is not a completed scientific test. Independent third-party replication is a separate qualification, never inferred from native replay, agent consensus or held-out cohorts.

The product of closure is an inspectable evidence package, including failures and limitations, not a promise of recognition.
