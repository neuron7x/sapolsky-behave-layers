# DGC Product v1 — Claim Boundary

Current authority: `RESEARCH_ARCHITECTURE_WITH_NARROW_VERIFIED_COMPONENTS`.

## Supported internal evidence

- prospective synthetic financial threshold: supported only on the frozen synthetic model;
- real two-exit local-model 30% quality-preserving target: not supported; declared workload oracle ceiling is below 30%;
- real repository release-triage mechanism: supported narrowly;
- combinatorial OOD inside the same validator/fault topology: supported narrowly;
- unknown validator domain: fail-closed abstention.

## Not currently supported

- validated external agentic-workload product effect;
- client-verified savings;
- live production-provider cost superiority;
- general superiority over strong routers;
- guaranteed 30% saving;
- production safety guarantee;
- independent replication;
- PRODUCT_QUALIFIED status;
- production control authority;
- ARR/ROI from hypothetical customers.

A product claim may be promoted only through the machine evidence stages and SHA-sealed evidence bundle. Internal mathematical or synthetic evidence cannot substitute for external workload evidence.
