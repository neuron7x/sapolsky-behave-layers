# CSCA-06A-R1 exploratory boundary

After the parent negative was known, a disjoint exploratory simulation was used to check whether global shared-nuisance profiling was worth a fresh preregistered experiment. Those exploratory outcomes are not stored as claim evidence and are excluded from the R1 cohorts. R1 uses untouched 151000/161000 seeds and retains the parent's alpha, cost ceiling, family definitions and power threshold.
