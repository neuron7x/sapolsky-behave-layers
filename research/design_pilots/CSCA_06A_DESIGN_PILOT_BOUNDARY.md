# CSCA-06A design-pilot boundary

A pre-preregistration simulation pilot was used only to determine a practical block size for the deliberately conservative composite-null e-process. Its seeds are permanently disjoint from confirmatory cohorts and its numbers cannot support the scientific verdict.

The confirmatory block size, alpha, nuisance envelope, alternative mixture, families, budgets, metrics and failure predicates are frozen in `research/preregistration/CSCA_06A_INTERVENTIONAL_FALSIFIABILITY.md` before PRIMARY/REPLICATION execution.
