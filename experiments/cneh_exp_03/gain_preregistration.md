# CNEH-EXP-03B — gain-path intervention and delivery qualification

**Prospective amendment, separate exploratory cohort.** Motivated by the completed EXP03 A1 outcome: eight of eight worlds showed temporal count contrasts despite zero terminal count contrasts; the harmonic probe had a nonzero discrete DC component. This new pilot is specified after EXP03, but before any EXP03B observations. Do not replace EXP03 endpoints, seeds, results or evidence.

## Design
Eight new public world seeds, eight arms per world: structural SHAM versus directed A-to-B CUT, crossed with no probe versus bounded DC-balanced A probe, crossed with endogenous gain versus a clamp to each world's common pre-intervention gain. Use the same 32-neuron reference simulator, topology, 0.1 ms step, 1000 ticks, intervention at 400. Match all exogenous random draws across arms. The two partitions have the same E/I composition as EXP03. All arms run identical pre-intervention dynamics. The gain clamp overwrites the actual scalar used in Network.step before each post-intervention transition, while allowing internal gain estimator updates to continue. Record both the applied and proposed gains. Do not clamp the recurrent weights or conductance memory except for the declared A-to-B edge deletion.

Subtract the discrete post-window probe mean and rescale the residual to match the original probe deviation L2. Verify bounds, DC balance and norm before execution. This is not matched electrical energy. Keep direct B external input identical; the endogenous gain contribution to the actual current is measured separately. No adjustment based on outcomes.

## Observables and falsifiers
Record per-tick full per-neuron spike bit vector, population B voltage, exact physical-state and RNG digests, external currents, applied and proposed gain, and the A-source/B-target conductance arrivals separately for E and I. Use the actual pinned weight matrices and previous-tick spike vector. Independently reconstruct the full three conductance updates from an independently copied RNG state and scalar decay equations, compare with actual post-step values within predeclared tolerances, and check RNG advancement and current-buffer equality. Reject a cut with nonzero direct post-cut A-to-B arrivals. Conductance arrivals are new input increments, not the full residual conductance or an isolated causal path effect. Record whether intact arms actually exercised nonzero A-to-B arrivals; no outcome-dependent exclusion.

## Estimands
For each gain policy, d(t)=B_SP(t)-B_SB(t)-B_CP(t)+B_CB(t). Report the maximum absolute cumulative contrast (population spike counts), total count contrast, and mean B voltage contrast. Report the time-aligned difference of d(t) between gain policies and per-neuron contrasts. Keep all signed individual world observations, including nulls. These are descriptive diagnostics, not confirmatory tests or natural direct/indirect mediation. Clamping gain does not eliminate alternative recurrent paths. Neither gain-policy dependence nor gain-policy invariance establishes a unique causal mediator. A fresh confirmatory design with a justified task, resource comparison, uncertainty calibration and independent replication is still required.

## Acceptance
Frozen protocol checked before execution. Every expected world/arm executes or reports explicit failure. Require exact pre-intervention traces and paired exogenous RNG histories, actual gain/current checks, independently reconstructed conductances, structural mask witness and replay-backed manifests. Record the scoped source lock and all negative controls. Missing or nonfinite values fail closed. No favorable neural effect required for engineering acceptance.
