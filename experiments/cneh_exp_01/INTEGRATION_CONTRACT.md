## Logical disposition

The original success rule requires every primary lower bound, including the contrast
with CENTRAL_EMULATOR, to exceed 0.05. Exact emulator parity implies paired differences
are identically zero, hence its Hoeffding lower bound is strictly negative for every
finite sample size. Completing physical measurements, richer substrates and external
replication cannot make that conjunction satisfiable. This lineage cannot authorize
SUPPORTED_NARROW. A restricted-comparator question requires a separately preregistered
lineage, new untouched cohorts and a substantive justification of the restriction.

Bitwise equality checks an implementation invariant, not the physical cost premise of
simulation dominance. Mathematical emulability alone does not prove equal energy or
latency on distributed and centralized hardware. These premises must remain explicit.
