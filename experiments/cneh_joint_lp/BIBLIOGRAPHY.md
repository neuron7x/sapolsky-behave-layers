## Decision hierarchy (not a ranking of universities)

1. Soundness before tightness: prove F maps into every admitted outer relaxation. Exact certificate replay (M01/M02/M07).
2. Existence before universality: retain constructive physical witness requirements from I. Exact UNSAT vs delta-SAT/UNKNOWN semantics (M03).
3. Decision-relevant inference before model identification: lower/upper regret and all competing actions, not unique minimax or pairwise agreement alone (M04/M05). Prior art must be credited.
4. Empirical claims require actual external data/model outputs and scoped uncertainty (M06). Existing REAL-TRANSFER-01 protocol and amendments remain frozen; no substitution of US datasets or thresholds. Data intake pass is not behavioral pass.
5. Continuity before additional complexity: agents consume machine-readable context, kernel-check proofs and reproduce artifacts (M07–M09). No new architecture until these obligations are tested.

## Remaining mathematics

Shared physiological nuisance parameters remain correlated across time; the LP uses outward coefficient intervals and does not assert equality with the nonlinear feasible set. Next candidate: branch on gamma/delay/tau and prove all subdomains covered, or prove nonlinear infeasibility with an independently checked interval/SMT result. Compare under equal proof-check and proposal budgets. A learned or heuristic proposer may accelerate search only; it cannot create epistemic authority. No guarantee of global mathematical novelty, physical calibration, independent external replication or deployment readiness.
