## Q2 — conditional intervention rule (design only; blocked until admission)

At action time t, propagate EXP05I admissible grammar states (including switch eligibility) from public history. The observation at t describes transition t-1, not m[t]. Under unrestricted hidden switching there is no guaranteed current-mode singleton after t=0; do not force one. Use an explicitly frozen grammar with true-containing support or abstain. Keep alternative hypotheses alive when transition uncertainty makes them feasible again; do not equate old exclusions with permanent evidence.

Use the actual intervention cost, not pulse count or physical energy: Q(a)=sum over source neurons and ticks of |I_i| dt. For the frozen source menu, four sources x 500 pA x 0.1 ms per stimulated tick = 200 pA ms (0.2 pC). This excludes fixed receiver bias and is a charge proxy, not joules or metabolic energy. Current budgets are caps, not obligations to spend. Include WAIT (zero incremental source charge) as a separate admissible choice; no division by zero. Use common wall-clock comparison horizon, same exogenous assumptions and identical public initial history.

Construct public-only uncertainty sets for source voltage and adaptation w, including erased spikes, from a qualified forward model; the existing shadow is approximate, not an exact native state. Propagate w continuously during both rest and stimulation; no reset and no use of archived latent w in the controller. Bound feasible spike and arrival count sequences under each candidate action, including silence, structural alias and bounded sensor error. If the model is not calibrated/qualified or any feasible outcome is omitted, the predicted information guarantee is unavailable. No assumed independent erasures or uniform prior.

At common horizon h, for each feasible coupled exogenous scenario xi, compute action-time projected support S_a(h,xi) and S_WAIT(h,xi) by public observation updates and grammar propagation. These are outer supports; each must contain the corresponding true mode, not a post-hoc subset. Define ambiguity A(S)=|S|(|S|-1)/2, an integer count of unordered unresolved mode pairs. Conservative incremental information proxy G(a)=min_xi [A(S_WAIT(h,xi))-A(S_a(h,xi))]. It is NOT Shannon mutual information. Use exact rational ranking G(a)/Q(a) for positive-charge candidates, only if G(a)>0, complete scenario coverage is certified, charge is within remaining budget and robust physical constraints pass. Re-evaluate at each declared decision time with new public observations and propagated w. Deterministic tie break: lower charge, then frozen menu order. Any expected-information alternative requires a separately preregistered calibrated observation/transition probability model and proper scoring; do not silently replace the robust proxy.
